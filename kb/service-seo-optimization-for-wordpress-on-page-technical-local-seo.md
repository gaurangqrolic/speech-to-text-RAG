### What does your WordPress SEO optimization service include?

Our service encompasses on-page optimization (keyword research, content optimization), technical SEO (site speed, mobile-friendliness, schema markup), and local SEO (Google My Business optimization, local citations) to improve your WordPress site's search engine rankings.

### How will on-page SEO improve my WordPress site?

On-page SEO involves optimizing your website's content and HTML source code. This includes keyword targeting, meta description optimization, header tag optimization, internal linking, and ensuring high-quality, relevant content that search engines can easily understand.

### What is technical SEO and why is it important for WordPress?

Technical SEO focuses on improving the underlying technical aspects of your website. This includes optimizing site speed, ensuring mobile-friendliness, creating an XML sitemap, implementing schema markup, fixing crawl errors, and ensuring proper site architecture. A technically sound website is more easily crawled and indexed by search engines.

### What is local SEO, and how does it benefit my business?

Local SEO focuses on optimizing your website to attract local customers. This includes optimizing your Google My Business profile, building local citations, targeting local keywords, and obtaining local reviews. Local SEO helps your business appear in local search results and maps.

### How long does it take to see results from your SEO services?

SEO results vary depending on factors such as website age, competition, and current rankings. Generally, noticeable improvements can be seen within 3-6 months with consistent effort. Long-term strategies are crucial for sustained success.

### How do you conduct keyword research?

Our keyword research involves identifying relevant keywords that your target audience uses when searching for products or services like yours. We use various tools and techniques to find keywords with high search volume and low competition. We also analyze competitor keywords to identify new opportunities.
