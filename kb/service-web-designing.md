## Website Design _Services FAQs_

Our experienced team of creative designers and developers delivers reliable, high-performance websites—no shortcuts, just quality that drives results.

### What web design services do you offer?

We offer custom website design services across multiple platforms including WordPress, Wix, Webflow, and fully custom-coded websites. We also specialize in converting PSD, Adobe XD, Figma, and AI designs into responsive websites.

### Which platforms do you work with?

Our team designs websites on WordPress, Wix, Webflow, and custom frameworks (HTML/CSS/JS). We recommend the platform based on your business needs, scalability goals, and content structure.

### How long does it take to design a website?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and number of pages. A typical business website takes 2–6 weeks from initial brief to launch. You’ll receive a detailed timeline after we understand your goals.

### Will my website be mobile-friendly and responsive?

Yes. Every website we design is fully responsive, ensuring it works seamlessly across all screen sizes — including smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

### Can you help with SEO in my website design?

Absolutely. We follow SEO best practices while designing your site — including clean code, fast loading times, optimized layouts, and proper HTML structure. We can also integrate your keywords and metadata if provided.

### Can I update the website myself after it’s live?

Yes. We build websites with easy-to-manage dashboards, especially in WordPress, Wix, and Webflow. You’ll be able to update content, images, and blog posts without technical knowledge.

### Do you provide website redesign services too?

Yes, but this page is focused on new website design. If you’re looking to refresh or rebuild an existing website, check out our Website Redesign Services.

### Do you offer ongoing website maintenance and support?

Yes, we provide optional website maintenance and support plans to ensure your site remains secure, up-to-date, and high-performing after launch.

### How much does a custom web design project cost?

Our pricing depends on your goals, required features, and platform. We offer affordable and scalable pricing, tailored for startups, SMBs, and enterprises. Contact us for a free consultation and estimate.

### Can you design websites for specific industries?

Yes. We have experience designing websites for healthcare, finance, education, eCommerce, real estate, and many more industries. We create designs that suit your niche and audience.
