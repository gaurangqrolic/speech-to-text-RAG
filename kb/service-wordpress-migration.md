Explore our frequently asked questions to learn more about Qrolic Technologies' WordPress migration services.

### What types of websites do you migrate to WordPress?

We migrate a variety of websites, including blogs, e-commerce sites, business websites, portfolios, membership sites, forums, static HTML sites, and sites from other CMS platforms like Drupal, Joomla, Wix, and Squarespace.

### What information do I need to provide for a quote?

Please provide your current website URL, the platform it’s on, the estimated number of pages and media files, any specific features or plugins you need migrated, and your desired timeline.

### How much does WordPress migration cost?

Costs vary based on website complexity and features. We offer transparent pricing and free quotes tailored to your needs. Contact us for a personalized estimate.

### What is your typical timeline for migrating a website?

Simple migrations can take about a week, while more complex sites may require more time. We’ll provide a realistic timeline after assessing your project.

### Do you offer post-migration support and maintenance?

Yes, we offer ongoing support and maintenance packages to ensure your site runs smoothly after the migration, including updates, security checks, and performance optimization.

### Do you handle SEO optimization during migration?

We account for SEO during the migration to minimize impact on your rankings and offer additional SEO services post-migration.

### Do you offer backups and disaster recovery for migration projects?

Yes, we create comprehensive backups before, during, and after migration to ensure data safety and easy recovery if needed.

### What security measures do you take during migration?

We use secure transfer protocols, regular backups, and temporary staging environments to ensure your data remains secure throughout the process.

### Do you offer a free consultation for migration projects?

Absolutely! We offer free consultations to discuss your project and answer any questions you have. Book a consultation on our website.
