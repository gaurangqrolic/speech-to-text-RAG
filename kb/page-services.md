### What services does Qrolic Technologies offer?

Qrolic Technologies offers a comprehensive range of services including web designing, web development, WordPress maintenance, WordPress migration, eCommerce/WooCommerce development, Block Theme (Gutenberg) theme development, technical architecture consulting, custom plugin development, and performance optimizations.

### How long has Qrolic Technologies been in business?

Qrolic Technologies was established in 2018\. Over the past years, we have grown significantly and built a strong reputation for delivering high-quality digital solutions.

### What sets Qrolic Technologies apart from other agencies?

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and client satisfaction sets us apart. We focus on building trust, fostering continuous learning, and maintaining a collaborative and friendly work environment. Our diverse team of experts ensures we can tackle any challenge with creativity and precision.

### How do you ensure the security of the websites you develop?

Security is a top priority at Qrolic Technologies. We implement best practices in web security, including regular updates, secure coding practices, and thorough testing. We also offer ongoing maintenance to keep your website secure from emerging threats.

### Can you help with both small and large projects?

Yes. Every website is built SEO-ready from day one — correct structure, meta tags, page speed, and mobile optimisation. We also offer ongoing SEO, Google Ads, and social media marketing as separate services for businesses that want to grow traffic after launch.

### Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive ongoing support and maintenance services to ensure your website remains up-to-date, secure, and performs optimally. Our support includes regular updates, performance monitoring, and quick resolution of any issues that arise.
