## Web Development Services _FAQs_

Our expert development team delivers robust, scalable websites built for performance—no shortcuts, just clean code and solutions that drive real results.

### What web development services do you offer?

We offer custom web development services using platforms like WordPress, Webflow, and fully hand-coded solutions (HTML, CSS, JS, PHP). We also specialize in converting Figma, PSD, XD, and AI files into functional, responsive websites.

### Which platforms and technologies do you work with?

Our development team works with WordPress, Webflow, and custom stacks including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and more. We choose the right technology based on your business goals, scalability needs, and budget.

### How long does it take to develop a website?

Timelines vary depending on complexity and features. A typical business website takes 3–6 weeks from planning to deployment. We provide a custom timeline after discussing your requirements.

### Will my website be responsive and mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every website we develop is fully responsive and tested across all devices—ensuring a seamless user experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

### Do you optimize for SEO during development?

Absolutely. We follow SEO best practices in development including clean, semantic code, fast load speeds, mobile optimization, and proper use of headings and metadata. We can also implement your keyword strategy if provided.

### Can I manage and update the website myself after it’s built?

Yes. We build websites with user-friendly backends—especially in WordPress and Webflow—so you can easily update text, images, and blog content without needing to code.

### Do you provide website redevelopment or migration services?

Yes. While this page focuses on new builds, we also offer redesign and migration services to improve outdated or underperforming websites. Visit our Website Redesign Services page to learn more.

### Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes, we offer flexible maintenance plans to handle updates, security patches, backups, and performance monitoring—ensuring your site runs smoothly after launch.

### How much does custom web development cost?

Costs vary depending on features, technology stack, and timeline. We provide transparent, scalable pricing with packages suitable for startups, SMBs, and enterprises. Contact us for a free quote.

### Can you develop websites for specific industries?

Yes. We’ve developed websites for industries including healthcare, eCommerce, finance, education, and more. We build solutions that align with your market and audience needs.
