## Overview

First Pass Airport is a **premium airport concierge service** offering luxury travel assistance, including **VIP meet & assist services and exclusive airport lounge** experiences. The platform is designed to **provide a seamless and high-end booking** experience for travelers seeking comfort, efficiency, and personalized service.

#### Our Approach

Our approach focused on delivering a luxury digital experience backed by smart automation.

We built the platform using a custom Gutenberg [block theme](https://qrolic.com/blog/move-to-wordpress-block-theme-2026-benefits/), ensuring flexibility and performance. At the core of the project was a custom booking system capable of handling complex pricing logic based on travel distance and service type.

By integrating Google Maps API, Stripe payments, and a custom workflow, we created a seamless and automated booking journey for premium users.

### Challenges

## Defining the Challenges

- Required a dynamic pricing system based on travel distance and vehicle/service type.
- Needed to integrate Google Maps API for accurate distance calculation.
- Ensuring secure and seamless payment processing with Stripe.
- Managing different booking flows (inquiry vs payment-based services).
- Eliminating manual pricing and reducing operational workload.

### The Solution

## Outcome-Driven Solutions

- Developed a custom WordPress website using Gutenberg block theme for flexibility and performance.
- Created structured Custom Post Types (CPTs) for Services and Testimonials.
- Built three distinct booking workflows:
- Inquiry-based booking for Signature Suite
- Payment-enabled booking for VIP Meet & Assist Heathrow
- Payment-enabled booking for VIP Meet & Assist Gatwick
- Integrated Google Maps Distance API for real-time distance-based pricing.
- Implemented Stripe payment gateway for secure and seamless transactions.
